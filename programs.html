How we work, in detail

Four stages of a renovation, costed before anything is committed.

Program 01 · Before anything is committed

Assessment & costing

Every renovation that overruns did so because the budget was set from a walkthrough. We inspect properly, cost the scope with trades rather than from a spreadsheet, and carry a contingency that reflects the age of the building rather than a flat ten per cent applied out of habit.

Program 02 · Pulled, not skipped

Permits & licensed trades

Unpermitted work is the cheapest decision available during a renovation and the most expensive one at resale, when it surfaces in a title search or an inspection. Permits are pulled where required, inspections attended, and the work signed off so it transfers cleanly.

Program 04 · Paperwork that transfers

Documentation & handover

A renovated property is worth more when the evidence exists: what was done, by whom, under which permit, with which warranty. Assembling it as the work happens costs nothing; reconstructing it two years later during a sale is often impossible.
