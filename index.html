Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I invest with you?

Not through this website. Nothing here is an offer or a solicitation, and no offering is made to anyone who reads it. We are not registered with the SEC or the Florida OFR.

Why is 'syndicate' in the name?

It is the registered company name. It does not mean an investment is being offered to the public on this site, and we would rather say so plainly than let the name imply otherwise.

Do you publish your returns?

No. Showing returns on a public website is exactly the kind of general solicitation that we do not engage in, and past results would not indicate future ones in any case.

Do you do the building work yourselves?

No. We are not licensed general contractors. Licensed trades do the work and permits are pulled where the work requires them.

Why does unpermitted work matter so much?

Because it surfaces later — in a title search, an inspection or an insurance claim — and by then it is the new owner's problem and the seller's liability.

Will you buy my property?

Possibly. Send the details and we will give you an honest answer, including when an agent would serve you better.

Got a property or a trade enquiry?

Send the details. This is not an investment enquiry line.
